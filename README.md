# Global-Superstore

In this repo, I will be doing **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** of the **Global Superstore dataset** available on Kaggle.

The main objective of this project is to analyse the dataset and identify **business insights that can support informed decision-making**.

I will follow a **first-principles approach**, where business questions are defined first and the data is then analysed to answer those questions.

## Analysis

The dataset contains **50K+ rows and 24 columns**, with information related to orders, customers, products, regions, segments, sales, quantity, profit and other business metrics.

The analysis will mainly focus on:

### 1. Segment-Wise Analysis

Comparative analysis of the three major segments:

* Consumer
* Corporate
* Home Office

Key areas include **sales, profit, profit margin, average order value and order performance**.

### 2. Category-Wise Analysis

Analysis of **category and sub-category performance** to understand:

* Sales and demand
* Profitability
* Contribution to overall profit
* High-sales vs low-profit products/categories

### 3. Region-Wise Analysis

Analysis of regional performance to understand:

* Sales and profitability across regions
* Category/sub-category performance by region
* Relationship between **lead time and profitability**
* Potential supply-chain and logistics optimisation opportunities

### 4. Time-Based Analysis

Analysis of sales and profit trends over time to identify **growth patterns, seasonality and changes in business performance**.

## Tech Stack

**Data Analysis:** Python, Pandas, NumPy
**Visualization:** Matplotlib, Seaborn
**Querying:** SQL
**Dashboard:** Power BI

## Project Objective

The goal of this project is not just to visualize the data, but to identify **meaningful business insights and actionable recommendations** that can help understand what drives sales and profitability and where improvements can be made.

After completing the EDA, I will build an **interactive Power BI dashboard** to present the key insights for business decision-making.
